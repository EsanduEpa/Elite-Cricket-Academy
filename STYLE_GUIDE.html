Design Principles

✨ Modern & Professional: Clean lines, proper spacing, and professional gradients

🎨 Consistent Colors: Blue and purple gradients with white backgrounds

🔄 Smooth Animations: Subtle transitions and hover effects throughout

📱 Responsive Design: Works perfectly on mobile, tablet, and desktop

♿ Accessible: Proper contrast ratios and readable typography

⚡ Performance: Optimized CSS with efficient rendering

Usage Guidelines

Headers: Use gradient backgrounds (#667eea → #764ba2) with white text

Cards: White backgrounds with subtle shadows (0 4px 20px rgba(102, 126, 234, 0.15))

Buttons: Gradient backgrounds matching the action type (primary, success, warning, etc.)

Icons: Gradient circle backgrounds with white icons inside

Spacing: Use 15px, 20px, 30px increments for consistent spacing

Border Radius: 12px for cards, 8px for buttons, 50% for circular elements

Shadows: Light shadows with blue tint (rgba(102, 126, 234, 0.15))
